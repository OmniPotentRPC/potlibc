POTLIB-2001

The POTLIB-2001 interface is described in detail in the Computer Physics Communications article accompanying the POTLIB-2001 program entry in the CPC program library.

The reference is:
R. J. Duchovic, Y. L. Volobuev, G. C. Lynch, D. G. Truhlar, T. C. Allison, A. F. Wagner, B. C. Garrett, and J. C. Corchado, "POTLIB:   A Potential Energy Surface Library for Chemical Systems", Computer Physics Communications 144, 169-187 (2002), 156, 319-322(E) (2004).
